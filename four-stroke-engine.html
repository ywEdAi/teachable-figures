04解释Explain

按顺序看图上的六处，每一步只亮起一个部分。Look at six places in order; each step lights up one part.

  1. 曲轴转一圈，活塞下去一次、上来一次：一圈只有两个冲程。One crank turn takes the piston down once and up once: a turn holds only two strokes.
  2. 进气门只在第一个冲程打开，排气门只在第四个冲程打开。第二、三冲程两个都关着。The intake valve opens only in the first stroke, the exhaust valve only in the fourth. In strokes two and three both stay shut.
  3. 火花塞只在压缩冲程的末尾点一次火，燃气把活塞推下去：这是唯一出力的冲程。The plug fires once, at the end of compression, and the burning gas drives the piston down: the only stroke that delivers work.
  4. 凸轮轮的直径是曲轴轮的两倍，所以凸轮只转半速：曲轴两圈，每个气门才开一次。The cam wheels are twice the crank wheel’s diameter, so the cams turn at half speed: each valve opens once per two crank turns.
  5. 四个冲程 × 180° = 720°。圆盘上的长指针走一圈，曲轴短指针走了两圈。Four strokes × 180° = 720°. While the long hand on the dial laps once, the short crank hand laps twice.
  6. 压力–容积图：做功冲程的曲线高、压缩冲程的低，两条之间围出的面积就是一个循环的净功。The pressure–volume chart: the power curve runs high, the compression curve low, and the area between them is the net work of one cycle.

1. 一圈只够两个冲程

活塞通过连杆挂在曲轴的偏心销上，所以曲轴每转一圈，活塞只能从上止点下到下止点、再回来。四个冲程（进气、压缩、做功、排气）各占半圈，合起来是 720°，也就是两圈。

2. 为什么不能每圈都点火

活塞往上走有两种用途：压缩新鲜混合气，或者把废气推出去。往下走也有两种：吸进新气，或者被燃气推着做功。几何上两圈一模一样，区别只在气门开没开、火花塞点没点。如果每圈都点火，排气冲程末尾缸里只有废气，点了也烧不起来。

3. 凸轮轴为什么是半速

每个气门在一个循环里只开一次，而一个循环是曲轴两圈，所以控制气门的凸轮必须每两圈转一圈。正时皮带上，凸轮轮的齿数是曲轴轮的两倍，就做到了这一点。修车时“对正时记号”也是因为这个：曲轴转一圈记号就回来了，凸轮要两圈。

4. 功从哪里来

同一个容积下，做功冲程的压力远高于压缩冲程（看压力–容积图）。燃气推活塞做的功，比压缩时花掉的多，差值就是一个循环的净功。进气和排气两个冲程几乎不出力，只是在换气。

1. One turn only holds two strokes

The piston hangs from an offset pin on the crankshaft through the connecting rod, so each crank turn can only carry it from top dead centre to bottom dead centre and back. The four strokes (intake, compression, power, exhaust) take half a turn each: 720° in all, two turns.

2. Why it can’t fire every turn

A rising piston can do two jobs: squeeze fresh mixture, or push exhaust out. A falling piston can also do two: draw in fresh mixture, or be driven by burning gas. The two turns are geometrically identical; what differs is whether the valves are open and whether the plug fires. Fire on every turn and the spark at the end of the exhaust stroke meets only spent gas, with nothing left to burn.

3. Why the camshaft runs at half speed

Each valve opens once per cycle, and a cycle is two crank turns, so the cams that push the valves must turn once every two crank turns. On the timing belt, each cam wheel has twice the crank wheel’s teeth, which does exactly that. It is also why mechanics “line up the timing marks”: the crank mark comes back after one turn, the cam marks only after two.

4. Where the work comes from

At any given volume the power stroke runs at far higher pressure than compression (see the pressure–volume chart). The gas pushes the piston harder on the way down than the piston pushed the gas on the way up, and the difference is the cycle’s net work. Intake and exhaust do almost no work; they only swap the gas.

power strokes per second = (rpm / 60) / 2

二冲程发动机呢？

摩托艇、链锯上的二冲程机确实每圈做一次功：它们不用气门，而是在活塞接近下止点时打开缸壁上的气口，一边排废气一边灌新气。代价是有一部分新鲜混合气会直接跑掉，所以更费油、更脏。

What about two-stroke engines?

Two-strokes on chainsaws and outboard motors really do deliver one power stroke per turn. They have no valves; ports in the cylinder wall open near bottom dead centre and swap exhaust for fresh mixture at once. The price is that some fresh mixture escapes straight out, so they burn more fuel and run dirtier.

这个模型的适用范围Where this model applies

气门正时按理想情况画：进气门正好在 0°–180° 打开，排气门正好在 540°–720°。真实发动机的气门会提前打开、推迟关闭，进排气门在上止点附近还会同时开一小段（气门重叠），点火也在上止点之前几度。

压力是理想奥托循环：进气、排气按大气压附近的常数，压缩和膨胀按多变指数 1.3，燃烧当作在上止点瞬间完成，峰值压力取压缩末压力的 3.2 倍。数值只用来比较大小。

尺寸是虚构的（缸径 84 mm、行程 72 mm、压缩比 10），不对应任何真实型号。剖面是正交投影，零件按比例；皮带画成前方的虚线，以免挡住剖面。

Valve timing is idealised: intake open from exactly 0° to 180°, exhaust from 540° to 720°. Real engines open valves early and close them late, keep both slightly open together around top dead centre (valve overlap), and fire a few degrees before top dead centre.

Pressure follows an ideal Otto cycle: intake and exhaust near atmospheric, compression and expansion with a polytropic exponent of 1.3, combustion treated as instant at top dead centre, and a peak of 3.2 times the end-of-compression pressure. The numbers are for comparison only.

Dimensions are invented (84 mm bore, 72 mm stroke, compression ratio 10) and match no real model. The cutaway is an orthographic projection with parts to scale; the belt is drawn as a dashed phantom in front so it doesn’t hide the section.
