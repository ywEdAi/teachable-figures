3Explain the relationship

Read the anatomical explanation

The lungs sit on either side of the heart in the chest. The left lung is slightly smaller because the heart takes up some space on the left. The diaphragm is a dome-shaped muscle below the lungs that separates the chest cavity from the abdominal cavity.

Rotating or hiding a structure changes visibility. It does not move an organ to a new position inside the body.

Scope. A simplified educational schematic, not a diagnostic model or a patient-accurate atlas. Shapes and spacing are illustrative. The front cover is an inspection aid, not a surgical cut. Lobes, vessels, pleura and heart chambers are omitted. No breathing or blood-flow simulation is shown.
