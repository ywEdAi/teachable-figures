03解释变化Explain what changed

看推理过程See the reasoning

由相似三角形得 r / R = h / H。这里 R = 4 cm、H = 8 cm，所以 r = h / 2。面积 A = πr²。h 加倍，r 也加倍；把加倍的半径平方，面积就变成 4 倍。

h = 2 cm 时，r = 1 cm，A = π cm²。h = 4 cm 时，r = 2 cm，A = 4π cm²。转动视角会改变你看到的形状，但不会改变这些数值。

Similar triangles give r / R = h / H. Here R = 4 cm and H = 8 cm, so r = h / 2. The area is A = πr². Doubling h doubles r, and squaring a doubled radius gives four times the area.

At h = 2 cm, r = 1 cm and A = π cm². At h = 4 cm, r = 2 cm and A = 4π cm². Turning the camera changes what you see, but never these values.

  1. 先看 h：切面从离锥顶 2 cm 移到 4 cm，距离加倍。Start with h: the slice moves from 2 cm to 4 cm below the apex, so the distance doubles.
  2. 再看拿掉的上半截：它和整个圆锥相似，所以半径 r 也加倍。Then the removed top: it is similar to the whole cone, so the radius r doubles too.
  3. 看金色切口：它始终是圆，只是转到斜处看起来像椭圆。Look at the gold cut: it is always a circle, even when it looks like an ellipse from an angle.
  4. 最后看面积：半径加倍再平方，2² = 4，所以是 4 倍。Finally the area: double the radius and square it, 2² = 4, so four times as large.

这个模型的适用范围Where this model applies

这是一个直圆锥，用平行于底面的平面去切。h 从锥顶量起，不是从底面量起。h = 0 时截面缩成一个点，面积为零；h = 8 时截面就是底面。这里不涉及斜截面，也不计算体积。

A right circular cone, cut parallel to its base. h is measured from the apex, not from the base. At h = 0 the circle is a point with zero area; at h = 8 it equals the base. No oblique sections or volume calculation are implied.
