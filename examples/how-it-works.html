04解释Explain

这个 skill 先决定学习者要改变什么、观察什么，然后才决定画什么。顺序是：

  1. 范围：没有可操作机制的请求（数据看板、整门课）在这里被挡下或改道。
  2. 交互模式：先问一轮，或按默认值直接做。
  3. 找机制：一个原因、一个结果、一个可能被推翻的误解。
  4. 选渲染：按主体选——示意图、iso-figure、Anatomy 机械套件，或来源网格。
  5. 教学规格：在画任何几何之前写好。
  6. 从一个模型构建：图和读数都只“观察”这个模型。
  7. 可操作，然后验证。

刚才拿掉的几步里，只有“选渲染方式”的失败能在截图里看出来。没有可改变的原因、没有预测、只能用鼠标、端点算错——这些情况下图都“看起来完成了”。所以最后一步是操作这张图来验证，而不只是看截图。

This skill decides what the learner will change and observe before it decides what to draw. The order is:

  1. Scope: requests with no mechanism to operate (dashboards, whole courses) are declined or redirected here.
  2. Interaction mode: ask one round of questions, or build from defaults.
  3. Mechanism: one cause, one effect, one misconception that can be disproved.
  4. Renderer: chosen by subject — schematic, iso-figure, the Anatomy mechanical kit, or source meshes.
  5. Teaching spec: written before any geometry.
  6. Build from one model: the picture and the readouts only observe it.
  7. Make it operable, then verify.

Of the steps you removed, only a skipped renderer choice shows up in a screenshot. With no cause to change, no prediction, mouse-only controls, or a wrong endpoint, the figure still looks finished. That is why the last step operates the figure instead of only looking at it.

“它先写 ___，再画 ___，因为一张只是看起来完成的图 ___。”“It writes ___ before it draws ___, because a figure that only looks finished ___.”

这个模型的边界What this model leaves out

这里的流程是 SKILL.md 的简化版：每个请求的决定是预先写好的，用来说明规则，不是真的在调用模型。真实使用时，agent 会读你的材料和项目，再做这些判断。

“拿掉一步”的后果是典型失败，不是每次都会发生的必然结果。这个 skill 产出的是设计良好的图，它们还没有经过学习者实验验证。

The flow here simplifies SKILL.md: each request's decisions are written in advance to illustrate the rules; no model is called. In real use, the agent reads your material and project before making them.

The effects of removing a step are typical failures, not guaranteed outcomes. The skill produces well-designed figures; they have not been tested with learners.
