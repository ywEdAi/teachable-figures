04解释，再预测一个新情形Explain, then predict a new case

在发射台上，地面可以支撑火箭。若推力不超过当前重量，火箭不会立即向上加速。离开发射台后，净加速度由 a = F/m − g 决定。

这个模型在燃烧时减少质量。低于初始重量的推力，有时会在燃料消耗后足以让火箭起飞。不能把“开始时没起飞”说成“永远不会起飞”。

燃料耗尽时，推力变为 0，向上的速度仍可大于 0。重力使它逐渐减速，达到最高点后再下降。图中落地后停止，不模拟碰撞。

On the pad, the ground can hold the rocket up. If thrust does not exceed the current weight, the rocket does not accelerate upward. Once it leaves the pad, net acceleration is a = F/m − g.

This model loses mass as it burns. Thrust below the starting weight can become enough for liftoff once some fuel is used. “It didn't lift off at first” does not mean “it never will.”

At burnout, thrust drops to 0, but upward velocity can still be positive. Gravity slows the rocket until it peaks, then it falls. The figure stops at touchdown; it does not model the impact.

模型、单位与边界Model, units and limits

这是一维教学模型，使用示意参数。干质量 800 kg，初始燃料 400 kg，最大推力 18,000 N，有效排气速度 1,800 m/s，重力加速度固定为 9.81 m/s²。燃烧流量为 ṁ = F / vₑ。滑块表示固定油门的一次试验，不是飞行中途改变油门。

高度和速度来自分段解析解，包含在台上消耗燃料、起飞、燃烧、熄火滑行与落地。忽略空气阻力、风、气压变化、地球曲率、分级和姿态控制。这不是轨道或真实运载火箭的性能预测。

查看物理来源

NASA Glenn 的火箭推力方程说明推力与排气流量、速度及压力有关。本例把压力贡献合并到有效排气速度中。NASA 的推重比说明与理想火箭方程提供模型的基础。数字均为教学选择。

This is a one-dimensional teaching model with illustrative parameters: dry mass 800 kg, initial fuel 400 kg, maximum thrust 18,000 N, effective exhaust velocity 1,800 m/s, and gravity fixed at 9.81 m/s². Mass flow is ṁ = F / vₑ. Each slider setting is one fixed-throttle trial, not a throttle change mid-flight.

Height and velocity come from a piecewise analytic solution covering fuel burned on the pad, liftoff, powered ascent, coasting after burnout, and touchdown. Drag, wind, changing air pressure, Earth's curvature, staging and attitude control are ignored. This is not an orbital or real launch-vehicle performance prediction.
