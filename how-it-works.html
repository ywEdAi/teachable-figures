teachable-figures turns one idea into a “predict, try, explain” interactive figure. This page is built the same way: hand it a few different requests, then remove a step and see what breaks. Watch the figure on the left: each step lights only the parts it is about and dims the rest. That is the attention layer the skill builds; the Focus guide switch turns it off.

04解释Explain

这个 skill 先弄清是谁在学、已经会什么，再决定学习者要改变什么、观察什么，最后才决定画什么。按下面的编号一步步看左边的图（不会自动播放）：

This skill first settles who is learning and what they already know, then what the learner will change and observe, and only then what to draw. Step through the numbers below and watch the figure (nothing plays by itself):

  1. 先读学习档案，问清给谁用：老师自己讲解，就做成插图或课堂练习；自学者没人搭支架，就先做背景探测。Read the workspace and ask who it is for: a teacher narrates, so build an illustration or class exercise; a self-learner has nobody scaffolding, so probe their background first.
  2. 范围：没有可操作机制的请求（看板、整门课）在这里被挡下或改道。Scope: requests with no mechanism to operate (dashboards, whole courses) stop or are redirected here.
  3. 至多五个问题，各带推荐答案；已知的不问；说“直接生成”就用默认值并列出假设。At most five questions, each with a default; nothing already known is asked; “just build it” uses defaults and lists assumptions.
  4. 先定机制和教学规格：一个原因、一个结果、一个要推翻的误解，以及预测阶段要藏起什么。Mechanism and spec come first: one cause, one effect, one misconception to disprove, and what the predict stage hides.
  5. 再按主体选渲染，从一个模型构建：图和读数只“观察”这个模型。Then pick the renderer for the subject and build from one model: picture and readouts only observe it.
  6. 可操作，然后真的去操作验证——截图只能发现少数失败。Make it operable, then operate it to verify: a screenshot catches few failures.
  7. 交付后更新学习档案；有了学习者证据，可选地用类型化补丁演化出 v2。After delivery, update the workspace; with learner evidence, optionally evolve a v2 through a typed patch.

每一步背后都有学习科学的理由：先接上学习者的已有图式；支架逐步撤掉，从带提示的练习走向独立练习；一次只改一个条件，控制认知负荷；预测时用聚焦把注意力引到该看的地方；文字标签紧挨着图形，悬停任一边，另一边跟着亮（双重编码）。试着把鼠标移到这些带虚线的词上。

Each step has a learning-science reason: connect to the learner's existing schema; fade the scaffolds from hinted practice to independent practice; change one condition at a time to keep cognitive load down; use focus at the predict stage to direct attention; put words right next to the picture, so pointing at either lights the other (dual coding). Hover the dotted terms to try it.

刚才拿掉的几步里，只有“选渲染方式”的失败能在截图里看出来。不了解学习者、没有可改变的原因、没有预测、只能用鼠标、端点算错——这些情况下图都“看起来完成了”。所以最后一步是操作这张图来验证，而不只是看截图。

这个模型的边界What this model leaves out

这里的流程是 SKILL.md 的简化版：每个请求的决定是预先写好的，用来说明规则，不是真的在调用模型。真实使用时，agent 会读你的材料和项目，再做这些判断。

“拿掉一步”的后果是典型失败，不是每次都会发生的必然结果。这个 skill 产出的是设计良好的图，它们还没有经过学习者实验验证。

The flow here simplifies SKILL.md: each request's decisions are written in advance to illustrate the rules; no model is called. In real use, the agent reads your material and project before making them.

The effects of removing a step are typical failures, not guaranteed outcomes. The skill produces well-designed figures; they have not been tested with learners.
