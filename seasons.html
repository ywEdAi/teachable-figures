04解释Explain

按顺序看图上的五处，每一步只亮起一个部分。Look at five places in order; each step lights up one part.

  1. 先看地面上的光束：太阳越低，同一束光摊得越开，每平方米分到的越少。Start with the beam on the ground: the lower the Sun, the wider the same light spreads, so each square metre gets less.
  2. 再看白天长度：夏天的白天更长，收集阳光的时间也更长。Then the daylight bars: summer days are longer, so they collect light for longer.
  3. 看轨道：地球一月最近、七月最远，最近的时候恰好是北京的冬天。Now the orbit: Earth is closest in January and farthest in July, and closest falls in Beijing’s winter.
  4. 看年度曲线：实线和虚线正好相反，两个半球的季节互相对调。Look at the year chart: the solid and dashed curves are mirror images, so the hemispheres swap seasons.
  5. 转动三维视图看地球：地轴一年到头指向同一方向；六月北半球朝太阳倾，所在纬度圈亮的部分超过一半，白天就超过 12 小时。Turn the 3D view and watch the globe: the axis points the same way all year; in June the north leans sunward, so more than half of your latitude circle is lit and the day lasts over 12 hours.
  6. 最后看侧视图：倾斜的地轴让太阳直射点在南北回归线之间来回。Finally the side view: the tilted axis swings the overhead Sun between the two tropics.

1. 太阳低，同一束光就摊得更开

北京正午的太阳，6 月 21 日高 73.5°，12 月 21 日只有 26.7°。同一束阳光照到地面，面积要乘以 1/sin(高度角)：夏天是 1.04 倍，冬天是 2.23 倍。每平方米分到的光，冬天只有夏天的一半左右。

2. 白天更长，光照时间就更长

同样两天，北京的白天是 14.8 小时和 9.2 小时。角度和时长叠加起来，6 月 21 日每天收到的阳光（大气层顶）约是 12 月 21 日的 3.1 倍。

3. 用“距离”来解释，会被两个事实推翻

地球在 1 月 3 日前后离太阳最近（0.983 AU），7 月 4 日前后最远（1.017 AU）。阳光强度随 1/r² 变化，只有约 ±3.4%，而且最强的时候正是北京的冬天。更关键的是：同一天、同一个距离，悉尼却是相反的季节。如果是距离造成季节，两个半球应该同时变冷变热。

4. 倾角决定季节有多强

太阳直射点在南北纬“倾角”度之间来回，所以回归线在 ±23.4°，极圈在 90° − 23.4° = 66.6°。倾角为 0，就没有季节；倾角越大，季节越极端。

1. A low Sun spreads the same beam thinner

Beijing’s noon Sun stands 73.5° high on June 21 and only 26.7° on December 21. One beam of sunlight covers 1/sin(elevation) as much ground: 1.04× in summer, 2.23× in winter. Each square metre gets roughly half as much light in winter.

2. Longer days collect light for longer

On the same two days Beijing has 14.8 and 9.2 hours of daylight. Angle and duration together give about 3.1 times as much sunlight per day (top of the atmosphere) on June 21 as on December 21.

3. Two facts sink the distance explanation

Earth is closest to the Sun around January 3 (0.983 AU) and farthest around July 4 (1.017 AU). Sunlight scales as 1/r², so it changes by only about ±3.4%, and it peaks during Beijing’s winter. More decisively: on the same day, at the same distance, Sydney has the opposite season. If distance caused seasons, both hemispheres would warm and cool together.

4. The tilt sets how strong seasons are

The overhead Sun swings between “tilt” degrees north and south, which is why the tropics sit at ±23.4° and the polar circles at 90° − 23.4° = 66.6°. With no tilt there are no seasons; the bigger the tilt, the harsher they get.

距离真的不重要吗？

对一年里的季节来说不重要，但在万年尺度上它很重要。地轴缓慢进动，让近日点在季节中移动，周期约 2.1 万年。大约 1 万年前，近日点落在北半球的夏天，北方夏季的日照比现在强得多。米兰科维奇认为，这类轨道变化调节了冰期的节奏。

Does distance never matter?

Not for the yearly cycle, but it does over millennia. Earth’s axis slowly precesses, moving perihelion through the seasons about every 21,000 years. Roughly 10,000 years ago perihelion fell in northern summer, and northern summers received noticeably more sunlight than today. Milankovitch proposed that such orbital changes pace the ice ages.

这个模型的适用范围Where this model applies

“每天的阳光”是大气层顶的日平均值，不含云、大气吸收、海洋和陆地的热容量。真实气温比日照晚约一个月达到峰值，所以最热的是七月下旬，而不是 6 月 21 日。

日出日落按太阳中心通过几何地平线计算，不含大气折射和日面半径，真实白天会长几分钟。日期按 365 天、近日点固定在 1 月 3 日计算，正午指太阳正午，不含时差。太阳的输出视为恒定（真实变化约 0.1%）。

轨道图为了看清，从斜上方投影，地球和太阳的大小都不按比例。

“Daily sunlight” is the daily mean at the top of the atmosphere; it leaves out clouds, absorption, and the heat capacity of oceans and land. Real temperatures peak about a month after sunlight does, which is why late July, not June 21, is hottest.

Sunrise and sunset use the Sun’s centre crossing a geometric horizon, without refraction or the Sun’s radius; real days run a few minutes longer. Dates use a 365-day year with perihelion fixed on January 3, and noon is solar noon, without the equation of time. The Sun’s output is held constant (it really varies by about 0.1%).

The orbit view is an oblique projection; Earth and Sun are not to scale.
